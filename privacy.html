Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short: Bonusik has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your cards stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud Keychain. We don't see them, sell them or share them. Photos are processed on the device and never uploaded.

This policy explains how the Bonusik app for iPhone ("Bonusik", "the app"), published by Superb ("we", "us"), handles information.

Your loyalty cards

The cards you add — store name, card number or barcode value, barcode type and colour — are stored in a database on your iPhone. A copy is saved in your iCloud Keychain so the cards can be restored if you reinstall the app on a device signed in with the same Apple ID. iCloud Keychain is end-to-end encrypted and operated by Apple; we have no access to it.

We do not operate any server that receives your cards, and we cannot see, recover or delete them for you.

Camera and photos

Bonusik asks for access to the camera to photograph a card or scan its barcode, and to your photo library so you can pick a picture or a store-app screenshot. Text and barcode recognition is performed entirely on your iPhone. Images are not uploaded anywhere and are not kept by the app after the card is recognised.

You can change these permissions at any time in Settings → Bonusik.

Sharing cards

When you share a card, the app creates a QR code or a file containing that card's details. It is sent only where you choose to send it (for example, a messenger app). Anyone who receives it can read the card's details.

Google ML Kit diagnostics

To recognise text and barcodes on the device, Bonusik uses Google ML Kit. ML Kit may send Google limited, anonymous diagnostic and usage information — such as device and app information, performance metrics and API usage — to help Google maintain and improve the recognition models. This information is not linked to your identity, does not include your photos or card numbers, and is not used for tracking or advertising. See Google ML Kit Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in or personal profiles.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics of our own.
  • No sale or transfer of your data to anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete a card in the app to remove it from the device and from the iCloud Keychain copy. Deleting all cards removes the backup contents as well. Deleting the app removes its local database; the Keychain copy remains until you delete the cards in the app or remove the entry from iCloud Keychain.

Children

Bonusik does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data about you, there is nothing we hold to access, correct or delete. If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK and have questions about your rights under the GDPR, contact us using the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
